# Vision

Jared Brain is a personal command center for a complex, ambitious life.

It should help Jared hold more context with less mental strain: business decisions, study, family, health, money, relationships, personal projects, memories, and AI-assisted execution should all have a clear place to land.

## Product Direction

The application should feel like an executive dashboard, not a note-taking landfill. It should surface what matters, reduce the cost of switching contexts, and preserve memory across time.

The first screen should answer:

- What matters now?
- What needs attention?
- What has changed?
- What should I remember?
- Where can AI reduce friction?

## Experience Goals

- Dark mode first
- Minimal but powerful
- Calm and premium
- Fast to scan
- Easy to return to
- Built for ADHD-friendly clarity
- Modular enough to grow for years

## Long-Term Capability Map

- Global capture from text, voice, links, files, and screenshots
- Memory timeline with semantic retrieval
- AI agents for briefing, planning, research, and synthesis
- Business dashboards across active ventures
- Study dashboards and concept memory
- Health and performance pattern tracking
- Relationship and family context
- Proactive insights and weekly executive reviews

## Product Ethic

Jared Brain should remain assistive, reviewable, and grounded. It should help Jared think, decide, remember, and act without creating a noisy second brain that becomes its own burden.
